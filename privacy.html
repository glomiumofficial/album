privacy policy · updated 13 october 2026

how glomium handles your data.

glomium manages social media accounts for its clients. our staff tool connects to instagram and facebook through meta's official apis, with the account owner's permission.

what we read

for accounts we manage: posts, comments, direct messages, page messages, follower counts and insights. when you message or comment on an account we manage, we see your public name, username, profile picture and what you wrote.

why

to reply to messages and comments, hide abusive comments, and report performance to the account owner. we do not sell data, run ads with it, or share it with anyone other than the account owner.

where and how long

data is stored on secured servers that only glomium staff can access. messages and comments are kept while we manage the account, and are deleted within 30 days after the engagement ends or when you ask.

deleting your data

see data deletion, or write to official@glomium.in.
